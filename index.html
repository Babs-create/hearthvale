Compass Realms

You start in Hearthvale, the walled city at the center of the world. A relic is hidden deep in each of the four lands around it. Bring all four back to the city fountain. The townsfolk know a thing or two, so stop and talk.

Move with WASD or arrow keys. Hold Shift to run. E talks to people. M opens the map. On a phone or tablet, drag anywhere to move. Lava burns, ice slides, and deep water blocks the way. Your health refills inside the meadows and the city.
