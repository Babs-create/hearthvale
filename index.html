Hearthvale

You start in Hearthvale, a big, crooked old city at the center of the world, from the stalls of Market Row to the back alleys of the Warrens. A relic is hidden deep in each of the four lands around it. Bring all four back to the city fountain. The townsfolk know a thing or two, so stop and talk.

Move with WASD or arrow keys. Hold Shift to run. E talks to people and uses things. I opens your inventory. M opens the map. On a phone or tablet, drag anywhere to move. Every game builds a new world with no roads through the wilds, so pick your own way. Lava burns, ice slides, and deep water blocks the way. Each land has its own creatures and hazards. Your health refills in the city, the meadows and at campfires in the wilds. If you collapse, you wake up in Hearthvale and drop some coins.
